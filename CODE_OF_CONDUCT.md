# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. Assume the person you are replying to is acting in good faith and knows things
you don't.

## What is expected

- Critique code, not people. "This drops the last write" is useful; "this is sloppy" is not.
- Take disagreement as information. Someone objecting to your design has usually noticed something.
- Accept that maintainers have limited time, and that "not now" and "not this" are legitimate answers.
- Respect that contributors are volunteers with jobs, families and time zones.

## What is not

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, sustained disruption, publishing others'
private information, or unwelcome attention of any kind. None of it is worth a contribution.

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and any other space where someone is representing
this project.

## Enforcement

Report a problem by opening an issue, or privately to the maintainer if that is not appropriate.
Reports are handled discreetly. Responses range from a clarifying reply to a permanent ban, in
proportion to what happened; the maintainer decides, and will explain the reasoning where doing so
does not expose the reporter.

If you are unsure whether something crosses a line, ask before posting rather than after.
